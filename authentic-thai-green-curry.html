Authentic Thai Green Curry Made Easy in 30 Minutes

Thai green curry combines aromatic herbs, creamy coconut milk, savoury seasoning, and gentle heat in one comforting dish. With prepared green curry paste and a few fresh ingredients, you can build a fragrant dinner without spending hours in the kitchen.

1 Choose a Good Curry Paste

Green curry paste provides much of the dish's character, so choose one with a flavour profile you enjoy. Different brands can vary considerably in heat, saltiness, and aromatic intensity, so taste and adjust the finished curry rather than adding large quantities immediately.

2 Build the Aromatic Base

Gently cooking the curry paste in a little oil helps release its aromas before the coconut milk is added. Take your time during this short stage and avoid burning the paste, as excessive heat can make the flavours harsh.

3 Add Coconut Milk Gradually

Coconut milk creates the creamy base that balances the curry paste. Add it gradually while stirring so the paste blends evenly into the liquid. Keep the heat moderate and avoid an overly aggressive boil.

4 Add Your Main Ingredient

Chicken is a popular choice, but tofu, prawns, or vegetables can also work well. Cut the main ingredient into evenly sized pieces so everything cooks consistently and absorbs the aromatic curry sauce.

5 Include Fresh Vegetables

Peppers, aubergine, green beans, bamboo shoots, and other vegetables can add colour and texture. Add firmer vegetables earlier and delicate ingredients later so they retain some bite rather than becoming soft.

6 Balance the Final Flavours

Thai-style curries rely on balance between salty, sweet, sour, spicy, and aromatic flavours. Taste near the end and make small adjustments rather than trying to correct everything with one ingredient.

7 Finish with Fresh Herbs

Fresh Thai basil or coriander can brighten the finished curry. Add herbs close to serving time so their aroma remains noticeable. Serve the curry with steamed jasmine rice to absorb the creamy sauce.

Conclusion

A satisfying Thai green curry can come together quickly when you focus on the right sequence. Cook the curry paste gently, build a creamy coconut base, add your protein and vegetables at the right time, then finish by balancing the flavours and adding fresh herbs.
