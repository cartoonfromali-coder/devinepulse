Perfect Pan-Seared Salmon with Herb Garlic Butter

Pan-seared salmon can be an impressive dinner without requiring complicated preparation. The key is to dry the fish properly, control the heat, and finish it with a simple garlic butter sauce that adds richness and fresh herbal flavour.

1 Dry and Season the Salmon

Before the salmon reaches the pan, pat the surface thoroughly dry with kitchen paper. Removing excess moisture helps the fish develop a better sear. Season both sides simply with salt and pepper and allow it to sit briefly while the pan heats.

2 Start with a Properly Heated Pan

Use a heavy frying pan that distributes heat evenly. Add a small amount of cooking oil and allow it to become hot before placing the salmon in the pan. A properly heated surface helps create colour without requiring excessive cooking time.

3 Cook the Skin Side First

If your salmon has skin, place it skin-side down first. Press gently for the first few moments to keep the skin flat against the pan. Allow it to cook undisturbed so the skin has time to become crisp and golden.

4 Finish with Garlic Butter

Once the salmon is nearly cooked, reduce the heat and add butter, minced garlic, and fresh herbs such as parsley or dill. Tilt the pan slightly and spoon the melted butter over the fish several times. This adds a fragrant finish without overwhelming the salmon.

5 Avoid Overcooking

Salmon continues cooking for a short time after leaving the pan, so avoid keeping it over direct heat longer than necessary. The centre should remain moist and tender rather than dry and flaky throughout.

6 Serve with Fresh Sides

A bright side dish can balance the richness of the butter sauce. Try steamed vegetables, a crisp salad, roasted potatoes, or fresh lemon. Keeping the sides simple allows the salmon to remain the main focus.

Conclusion

Perfect pan-seared salmon comes down to a few reliable techniques: dry the fish well, start with a hot pan, leave the skin undisturbed, and finish with aromatic garlic butter. Once you master those basics, this quick dinner becomes an easy addition to your regular meal rotation.
